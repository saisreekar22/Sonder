Before you start

EchoPath is a supplementary aid. It is not a replacement for a cane, a guide dog or sighted assistance.

It can miss obstacles, especially glass, stairs, drop-offs and anything in low light.

Silence means nothing was detected. It never means the way is safe.

Video is processed only on this phone. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.
