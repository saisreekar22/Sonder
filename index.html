    Before you start

    EchoPath is a supplementary aid. It is not a replacement for a cane, a guide dog or sighted assistance.

    It can miss obstacles, especially glass, stairs, drop-offs and anything in low light.

    Silence means nothing was detected. It never means the way is safe.

    Video is processed only on this phone. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

    Bluetooth headphones on a Windows laptop: fix steps
    1. Turn the headphones on. If they're connected to your phone, disconnect them there (or use their multipoint mode).
    2. Windows Settings, Bluetooth and devices: select your headphones, then Connect. Wait until it says "Connected music".
    3. Sound settings, Output: choose the entry called Headphones (your headphones), not Headset (… Hands-Free). Hands-free is mono, so left and right can't work.
    4. If Windows keeps switching to Hands-Free: Control Panel, Devices and Printers, right-click the headphones, Properties, Services, and untick Hands-free Telephony.
    5. Come back here and press Reconnect sound, then Circle me.
